Sure Wish I Had Some Bread

This project is a funny reaction meme with a cat and dog crying. I wanted to make a funny picture about feeling sad over something small. The cat and dog are crying because they want bread, which makes the situation look very serious. The picture shows how people can overreact to small problems.
